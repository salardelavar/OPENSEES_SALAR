# Welcome, a free educational resource hub for structural and earthquake engineers at every career stage. This blog features a growing collection of practical, ready-to-use scripts written in Python and OpenSees, covering a wide range of analysis, design, and optimization examples. Whether you are a student exploring numerical methods or a practicing engineer seeking to automate complex workflows, you will find clear, code-driven solutions designed to make advanced structural analysis more accessible. All content is provided free of charge, with the goal of supporting learning and knowledge-sharing across the engineering community.
